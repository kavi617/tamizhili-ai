Senguttuvan (Cheran Senguttuvan) is a legendary Chera king celebrated in the Sangam epic Silappatikaram (Chilapathikaram), attributed to Ilango Adigal. The poem blends history, myth, and moral tale around Kannagi’s justice.

Tradition claims Senguttuvan led a northern expedition to gather stones for Kannagi’s deification and fought Himalayan kings. Historians treat many details as literary amplification rather than verified campaign logs.

Silappatikaram describes trade routes, court life, and religious diversity in Tamilakam—Jain, Buddhist, and folk practices. It remains a foundational Tamil literary work taught in schools.

Senguttuvan symbolizes Chera martial glory in popular memory. Archaeology does not confirm every episode, but the epic preserves social customs, city names, and values of the early centuries CE.
